Why Quantum Computing Matters

  • Drug Discovery: Simulate molecular interactions at quantum scale, accelerating pharmaceutical research by years.
  • Cryptography: Quantum algorithms can break current RSA encryption — driving a global shift to post-quantum security.
  • Optimization: Solve combinatorial logistics, supply-chain, and financial modeling problems that are intractable classically.
  • Unlock a new paradigm of exponential computational advantage across science, industry, and security.

P. W. Shor, "Polynomial-Time Algorithms for Prime Factorization and Discrete Logarithms on a Quantum Computer", SIAM J. Comput., 1997.

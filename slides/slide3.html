Core Quantum Concepts

  • Superposition: a qubit exists in multiple states simultaneously — until it is measured and collapses to one.
  • Entanglement: two qubits become correlated so that measuring one instantly determines the other, regardless of distance.
  • Interference: quantum algorithms amplify correct paths and cancel wrong ones through wave-like probability interference.
  • Harness all three phenomena together to achieve computational speedups impossible classically.

M. A. Nielsen and I. L. Chuang, "Quantum Computation and Quantum Information", Cambridge University Press, 2000.

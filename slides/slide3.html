Core Quantum Concepts

Superposition

The ability of a quantum system to be in multiple states at the same time until it is measured.

  • Imagine spinning a coin; while it spins, it is both heads and tails.
  • Enables exponential parallel processing power.

Entanglement

A deep quantum connection between particles regardless of distance.

  • Measuring one instantly determines the state of the other.
  • Einstein called this "spooky action at a distance".
  • Crucial for quantum communication and cryptography.
